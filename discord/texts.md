## 5. #faq

**Frequently asked questions**

**What is Reminth?**
A free Minecraft: Java Edition launcher for Windows 10 and 11. It sets up Minecraft, mod loaders and Java for you, comes with performance mods and its own in-game panel with about 145 features.

**Is it free?**
Yes.

**Does Reminth see my Microsoft password?**
No. You sign in on Microsoft's own page (microsoft.com/link). Reminth never sees your password.

**Which Minecraft versions and mod loaders does it support?**
Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge. The Reminth Mods Panel works on every version from 1.20.1 to 26.3.

**Does it work on Mac or Linux?**
Not yet. Reminth is Windows only for now.

**How do I open the Reminth Mods Panel?**
Press **G** in game. You can change the key in Minecraft's Controls settings.

**What is the Tier Tagger?**
It shows players' PvP tiers from MCTiers, PvPTiers and SubTiers next to their names. It's on by default and you can change it in the panel under Visual. Type `/tiers <name>` in game to see all of a player's tiers.

**Can I get banned for using Reminth?**
Reminth's own features only show information about you or change how the game looks. Every server has its own rules though, and Reminth gives you a heads-up when a server's rules ban one of your mods. You're responsible for following the rules of the servers you play on.

**Do I need a Reminth account?**
Yes. Reminth asks you to make one the first time you open it. It's free, and you can use Discord or email. The same account works on the website.

**How do I get the Launcher Player role?**
Sign in to Reminth with Discord, then type `/verify` in #commands.

**How do I update Reminth?**
You don't need to. Reminth updates itself and tells you when a new version is ready.

**I found a bug or need help.**
Open a ticket in #support and we'll help you there.
