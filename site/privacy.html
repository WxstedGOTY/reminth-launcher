Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Version 3

The short version. Reminth has no servers, no accounts of its own, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. What the launcher stores stays on your computer, and we never receive it. The launcher talks directly to the services it needs (Microsoft, Mojang, Modrinth, GitHub and so on), listed below. The only personal data that ever reaches us is what you choose to send us, such as an email. You have full rights over that data under the GDPR, and you can complain to a data protection authority at any time. This summary is for convenience; the full text below is what applies.

Contact for anything in this policy: reminthsupport@gmail.com. We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer and have not done so.

2. The principle this policy follows

This page describes what the code actually does. Reminth is built so that we don't need your data: there is no Reminth server for the launcher to report to, no user account to create, no tracking, and no selling or sharing of personal data with anyone. We do not use your data for profiling or automated decision-making.

3. Personal data we process

a) Visiting this website

The site is a static page. It sets no cookies, uses no local storage, runs no analytics, trackers or advertising, and has no forms.

  • Hosting (Netlify). The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (USA), which acts as our processor. Like any web server, it receives your IP address, browser user agent, the page requested and the time, and keeps short-lived server logs. Purpose: delivering the site and protecting it from abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Retention: as set by Netlify for its logs; we do not export, combine or analyse them. Transfer outside the EU: covered by Netlify's data processing agreement and the safeguards it provides, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Fonts (Google). The main page currently loads its typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), which means your browser sends your IP address and user agent to Google LLC when the page loads. The legal pages load nothing external.
  • Downloading Reminth (GitHub). The download button points at a GitHub release. Downloading is a request to GitHub, Inc., under GitHub's own privacy statement. We can only see total download counts, not who downloaded.

b) Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. Purpose: answering you, and dealing with privacy requests, bug and security reports, legal or takedown notices. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in replying (Art. 6(1)(f)); where your email relates to a purchase, performing our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)); and complying with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)). Retention: we delete the conversation 12 months after it ends, unless we need to keep it longer to deal with a legal claim. Our email provider stores it on our behalf as a processor.

If you open an issue on the Reminth GitHub repository, it is public and handled by GitHub under its own terms. Don't post personal details there; email us instead. We will delete or hide issues we control on request.

4. The launcher: what stays on your computer

Everything the launcher saves lives on your own computer, mainly in %APPDATA%\Reminth. None of it is sent to us. We have no access to it.

  • Your sign-in tokens (account.json), so you don't have to sign in every time. They are encrypted with your Windows account's own key (DPAPI, via Electron's safeStorage) and are only ever sent to Microsoft and Mojang to keep you signed in.
  • Your settings and instances: memory, accent colour, and each instance's version, loader and options.
  • Skins: a cached copy of your current skin and any skins you save to your library.
  • Game logs: an archive of your game logs, so older sessions stay viewable in the Logs tab.
  • Caches of public information: a catalogue of public Modrinth listings, and which Modrinth project each of your installed files is and who made it (creator name, profile picture address, icon).
  • An update log (updater.log) recording update checks.
  • The game itself: Minecraft, mod loaders, Java, your mods and your worlds.
  • Streamer mode (only if you turn it on): screenshots and saved clips go to Videos\Reminth, and a short rolling recording buffer of the Minecraft window is kept in %APPDATA%\Reminth\replay-buffer until you save a clip or it is overwritten. It records only the Minecraft window, only while streamer mode is on and the game is running.

To fill in Home, your instances and Player Statistics, the launcher reads your worlds, server lists, statistics, logs and mod files in its own game folders. This happens locally.

5. The launcher: what it sends, and to whom

The launcher has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting or tracking. Each request goes directly from your computer to the service involved. We are not involved in these requests and never see them. Each service is responsible for what it does with the request under its own privacy policy. All of them can see your IP address, as with any request on the internet.

  • Microsoft and Xbox Live: to sign you in to your own Microsoft account, on Microsoft's own sign-in page. Reminth never sees your password.
  • Minecraft Services and Mojang: to verify that you own the game, load your profile and skin, look up another player's skin when you use “copy a skin by username”, and change your skin or cape when you press Save.
  • Mojang's download servers and Microsoft's Java download: to download the game and Java.
  • Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge: to download the mod loader you pick, and Fabric API from Fabric's own server.
  • GitHub: to check for and download Reminth updates, and to download the performance mods Reminth installs (Sodium, Lithium and ScalableLux) from each project's own GitHub releases.
  • Modrinth: to browse, search, install and update content. To show who made each mod and whether an update exists, the launcher sends Modrinth the file fingerprints (SHA-1 hashes) of the mods, packs and shaders in your instances. A fingerprint identifies a file, not you. Requests identify the app as “reminth-launcher” and its version; no account information is sent.
  • Image servers: mod icons and creators' profile pictures load from cdn.modrinth.com and, for creators who use a GitHub picture, avatars.githubusercontent.com.
  • Minecraft servers: when you view servers in the launcher, it contacts each server directly to show whether it's online, its player count and your ping, the same way the game's multiplayer screen does.

Mods you install are made by third parties and may make their own network requests once the game is running. Reminth's own HUD mod runs locally and sends nothing anywhere.

6. Paid features

Before any paid feature launches, this policy will be updated to say exactly what data it needs (for example, the details our payment provider needs to process your payment), who processes it and for how long, and you will see that before you pay.

7. Children

Playing Minecraft requires a Microsoft account, and Microsoft applies its own age rules and parental controls. Reminth does not create its own account, does not ask your age, and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are under 15, please ask a parent or guardian before emailing us. A parent or guardian who believes their child has sent us personal data can ask us to delete it.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict how we use it;
  • object to our use of it based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability, where it applies;
  • withdraw any consent you gave, at any time, without affecting what was done before.

Email reminthsupport@gmail.com to use any of these rights. It's free, and we answer within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests, in which case we'll tell you why). We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

Data that only exists on your own computer (section 4) is fully under your control: sign out to delete the stored account file, or delete the %APPDATA%\Reminth folder (and Videos\Reminth if you used streamer mode) to remove everything the launcher has written. Back up any worlds first, since this deletes them too.

Right to complain. You can complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Αρχή Προστασίας Δεδομένων Προσωπικού Χαρακτήρα), Kifisias 1-3, 115 23 Athens, Greece, www.dpa.gr, or to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work. We'd appreciate the chance to fix the problem first, but you don't have to contact us before complaining.

9. Security

Sign-in tokens are encrypted on your computer, every downloaded file is checked against the checksum its source publishes, and the launcher contacts only the services listed above over encrypted connections where the service supports them. No system is perfectly secure; if you find a security problem, please email us privately instead of posting details publicly.

10. Changes to this policy

When what Reminth does changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves. If a change affects your data in a significant way, such as a new feature that stores data off your computer, we will announce it in the launcher and on this site before it takes effect, not as a quiet edit.
