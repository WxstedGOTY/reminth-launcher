Fabric, Quilt, Forge & NeoForge

Compatible with the mods you actually use

On Fabric and Quilt instances Reminth adds Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast, Entity Culling and ScalableLux for you, plus the Fabric API they need — stable releases from Modrinth, only when a build exists for your Minecraft version. It leaves out any mod you already have or that clashes with yours, and you can switch the pack off per instance. Everything else installs from Modrinth inside the launcher. Here's a sample of what the Fabric ecosystem has to offer:

CompatibilityNew
No more “Incompatible mods”

Reminth checks your mods before the game starts, tells you which ones won't load and why, and can fix them in one click. It can also work out which Minecraft version fits all your mods and your server.
