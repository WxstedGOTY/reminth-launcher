Built on Fabric

Compatible with the mods you actually use

Reminth ships with Sodium, Lithium, ScalableLux and Fabric API already installed — real performance gains out of the box, no setup. It also runs vanilla Fabric underneath, so any other Fabric mod drops straight into your instance and just works. Here's a sample of what the Fabric ecosystem has to offer:
