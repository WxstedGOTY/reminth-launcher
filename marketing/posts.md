# Reminth posts - X and Reddit

Every feature named here is real (checked against the app and the website). Don't add claims like "+200 FPS" or
"better than Lunar" - one wrong claim and the comments turn on you.

## Rules so the accounts don't get banned

- **Attach a picture or a short clip to every post.** Text-only launcher posts get scrolled past.
- **Never post the exact same text twice.** X and Reddit both treat copy-paste posts as spam. Mix the posts below.
- **Reddit is strict with new accounts.** A brand-new account that only posts its own link gets auto-removed or
  shadowbanned. First few days: comment normally in Minecraft subreddits, post Reminth max **once a day**, and read
  each subreddit's rules (most only allow self-promo on certain days or in a weekly thread).
- **Say you made it** on Reddit ("I made this"). Hidden self-promo gets reported; honest dev posts get questions.
- Answer every comment and reply - that's what actually gets players.
