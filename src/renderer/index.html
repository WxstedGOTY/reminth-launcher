                Privacy Policy
                

                  
Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Version 8. This is a summary. The full policy is at
                    reminth.pages.dev/privacy.html
                    and is what applies.


                  
Who's responsible. Reminth is run by Lefteris Psilopanagiotis, an individual developer in
                    Greece, who is the data controller under the GDPR. Contact: reminthsupport@gmail.com.


                  
What stays on your computer. Your Microsoft sign-in tokens are stored encrypted by
                    Windows, with your Windows account's own key. If Windows encryption isn't available, they are not
                    saved at all and you sign in again next time. Reminth keeps its files in
                    %APPDATA%\Reminth: your settings, instances, saved skins, caches of
                    public Modrinth information, and logs (updater.log,
                    auth.log, main-errors.log, the last game
                    log and the archive of your older game logs). Streamer mode's recording captures the Minecraft
                    window's picture and, when sound is on, everything the PC plays; it keeps a short rolling
                    recording there and saves clips and screenshots to Videos\Reminth.
                    None of this is ever sent to us. When it starts, Reminth also looks at the list of running Java
                    programs on your PC (only their process number, start time and game folder, never the sign-in
                    token in their start command) so it can recognise a game it started earlier; that stays in memory.
                    The in-game bar (ReminthHUD) reads your CPU and graphics load from Windows' own counters and
                    sends nothing to us. On a multiplayer server it also asks that server for your own statistics (the
                    same request the game's Statistics screen makes) and saves the answer on your PC, so Player
                    Statistics can include your server numbers. The Reminth title screen in the game stores and sends
                    nothing.


                  
What Reminth sends, and to whom. No backend, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or
                    tracking. The launcher talks directly to: Microsoft and Xbox Live (sign-in); Mojang and Minecraft
                    Services (licence check, game and Java downloads, skins and capes); Fabric, Quilt, Forge and
                    NeoForge (the loader you pick, and Fabric API); GitHub (Reminth updates, and Sodium, Lithium and
                    ScalableLux from their own releases only if Modrinth can't be reached); Modrinth (Discover, installs,
                    the performance pack,
                    updates and the compatibility check, which sends the SHA-1 file fingerprints of your mods —
                    they identify files, not you); the hosts a modpack lists for its files (Modrinth, GitHub or GitLab);
                    cdn.modrinth.com and avatars.githubusercontent.com (icons, creator pictures, and pictures in project
                    descriptions and galleries; pictures from any other host are never loaded); and Minecraft
                    servers you look at (status and ping). Each can see your IP address, as with any internet request.
                    Links on a project page open in your own web browser, not in Reminth.


                  
What reaches us. Only what you choose to send, such as an email. We delete support
                    conversations 12 months after they end.


                  
Your rights. You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how it's used,
                    by emailing reminthsupport@gmail.com. You can complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority
                    (dpa.gr) or your own country's authority. To remove
                    everything the launcher stored, sign out, then delete %APPDATA%\Reminth
                    (and Videos\Reminth). Back up your worlds first.
