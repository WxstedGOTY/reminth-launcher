Privacy & terms

              
The short version: Reminth has no servers of its own, so there is nowhere for
                your data to be sent. Everything it stores stays on this computer.


              

                Privacy Policy
                

                  
Last updated: 23 September 2026


                  
What Reminth stores on your computer. Your Microsoft sign-in tokens are saved to
                    %APPDATA%\Reminth\account.json, encrypted with your Windows account's
                    own key (DPAPI, via Electron's safeStorage). Your settings, instances, a cached copy of your
                    skin, the skins you've saved, a permanent archive of your game logs, and a local catalogue of
                    public Modrinth listings are stored in the same folder. Clips and screenshots from streamer
                    mode are saved to Videos\Reminth. Signing out deletes the stored
                    account file.


                  
What Reminth sends, and to whom. Reminth has no backend, collects no analytics, and
                    contains no telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking. It only talks directly to the
                    services needed to do its job: Microsoft and Xbox Live to sign in; Mojang and Minecraft
                    Services to verify your account, download the game and Java, and — only when you press Save
                    on a skin or cape — change your skin; Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge for the mod loader you pick; Modrinth's public API for
                    Discover, installs and update checks; and, when you look at the Servers tab, the servers
                    themselves, to measure your ping to them the same way the game's multiplayer screen does.


                  
Your game files. Reminth reads your worlds, server lists, statistics and logs from its
                    own game folders to fill in the app. That reading is local — none of it is uploaded anywhere.
                    Streamer mode records only the Minecraft window, only while streamer mode is on and the game
                    is running, and keeps just the last few minutes on disk until you save a clip.


                  
Children. A Microsoft account is required to play Minecraft, and Microsoft applies
                    its own age rules at sign-in. Reminth adds no separate account of its own.


                  
Deleting your data. Sign out to remove the stored account, or delete the
                    %APPDATA%\Reminth folder to remove everything Reminth has ever
                    written. There is no server-side copy to request, because there is no server.
